    Game plan

    • Rotisserie with 12 skater categories, so HIT, BLK, PIM and ATOI count as much as goals. Tkachuk, Wilson and Seider types are worth more here than their ESPN ADP.
    • Goalies: two Solid starters first (the goalie watch says when), then two options: Fighting for the job, then Split, then Backup. Drop a failed option for a waiver goalie.
    • Pick a goalie strategy once you see your first two: Balanced, Volume (W, SO) or Ratio (GAA, SV%). Stay above the 60-start minimum for the ratios to count.
    • Defencemen score in BLK, ATOI and PPA. An elite two-way D (Makar, Bouchard, Dahlin, Werenski, Seider) in rounds 1–3 is a real edge.
